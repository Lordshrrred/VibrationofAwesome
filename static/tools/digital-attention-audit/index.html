Reflection Tool

Digital Attention Audit

A non-clinical reflection tool for noticing which digital inputs are draining focus, self-trust, and creative energy.

Important: this is an educational reflection tool, not a medical, psychological, or diagnostic instrument.

1. I reach for a screen before I know what I am actually feeling.

2. My attention feels split even when I have time to focus.

3. Digital inputs make it harder to hear my own creative signal.

4. I use scrolling, feeds, or videos to avoid a decision I already know I need to make.

5. After being online, I often feel flatter, foggier, or less self-trusting.

6. My tools are controlling the rhythm of my day more than my actual priorities are.

What to do with the result

Use the score as a signal, not a verdict. Pick one digital input to reduce for seven days, then replace that slot with a physical cue: a walk, a notebook, one song, one breath practice, or one unfinished creative action.
