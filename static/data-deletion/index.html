Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: June 6, 2026

EarthStar Command is a private/local analytics tool for owned creator accounts connected to Vibration of Awesome.

To request deletion of locally cached analytics data associated with EarthStar Command, email:

matt@vibrationofawesome.com

Subject line: EarthStar Command Data Deletion Request

Include:

  • your name
  • the Facebook Page or Instagram account involved
  • a short description of what data you want deleted

Matt will delete applicable locally cached analytics data where possible.

You can also remove the app's access from your Facebook, Instagram, or Meta account settings.

The app does not sell user data. The app does not publish, schedule, upload, comment, message, DM, or write to Meta.
