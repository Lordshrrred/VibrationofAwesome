Jewelry archive

This page is a visual history of the jewelry work, gathered piece by piece rather than treated like an active storefront.

Nothing here is for sale. The goal is simply to preserve the shape of the work, the craftsmanship, and the evolution of the style over time.

Archive Disclaimer

Some pieces still carry restored listing notes or naming context from the Etsy era. Others are shown more quietly for now while the archive gets cleaned up.

Each cover image has been re-centered around the jewelry itself so the work reads clearly in the grid before you open the full piece view.
