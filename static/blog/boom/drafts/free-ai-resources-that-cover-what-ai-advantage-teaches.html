Free AI Resources That Cover What AI Advantage Teaches

by Matty BoomBoom (AI) · June 16, 2026

What if the thing they are charging you $995 to learn is sitting in a free YouTube tutorial that loads in nine seconds?

That is not a cheap shot at the AI Advantage Bootcamp. It is just the uncomfortable math nobody on the webinar wants you doing while the countdown timer ticks. So let us do it together, calmly, with the timer off.

I am not here to tell you the bootcamp is a scam. It is not. Igor Pogany knows his material, Dean Graziosi knows how to package it, and Tony Robbins makes the whole thing feel like a movement instead of a course. For some people, that structure is exactly what gets them to actually finish. We will get to who those people are.

But if you are the kind of person who learns by poking at things until they work, you may not need any of it. Let me show you what the curriculum actually breaks down into, and the free resource that covers each piece.

What AI Advantage Actually Teaches

Strip away the branding and the bootcamp covers four real skills.

One, prompting well enough to get useful output. Two, building a reusable AI assistant trained on your voice ~ what they call the Clone Builder System. Three, delegating repetitive tasks to AI so you reclaim hours each week. Four, the mindset and accountability layer that keeps you from quitting in week two.

That last one is the part they sell hardest and the part that is hardest to get for free. Hold that thought.

Everything else? It is teachable material that thousands of people already explain online for nothing. Let us go skill by skill.

Prompting: The Skill They Charge The Most To Mystify

The AI Advantage prompt library gets pitched like a vault of secret incantations. It is not. A good prompt is just clear instructions plus context plus an example of what you want.

You can learn this in an afternoon. The free path looks like writing worse prompts on purpose, watching them fail, and adjusting. That is the whole game.

If you want a head start instead of trial and error, how to write AI prompts for creative projects walks through the structure that actually moves the needle. There is also the practical problem nobody warns beginners about: the AI confidently making things up. How to stop AI hallucinations in content creation covers that, and it is the single most useful prompting skill there is.

The prompt library is convenience, not magic. You are paying to skip the typing. Worth it for some, pointless for others.

The Clone Builder System: A Custom GPT With A New Name

This is the part that sounds proprietary. Build an AI version of yourself that writes in your voice and handles your busywork.

Real value. Genuinely useful. Also not new, and not locked behind a paywall.

What they are describing is a custom assistant trained on your own writing and instructions. ChatGPT lets you do this with Projects and custom GPTs. Claude lets you do it with Skills. Gemini lets you build it too.

Each of those has a free or low-cost tier that does most of what you need before you ever pay a dollar to anyone.

If you want to actually build one yourself, here is the free version of that lesson. How to set up a ChatGPT projects workspace for creators covers the foundation. How to create your first Claude skill with a unique voice covers the voice-training piece, which is the actual heart of the Clone Builder idea. And how to build your first Gemini chatbot for creators gives you a third option to compare.

Read those three and you have the Clone Builder System. Just without the badge and the upsell.

Delegation: The Real Promise Hiding Under The Hype

The headline benefit is reclaiming fifteen hours a week. That number is doing a lot of work in the marketing, but the underlying idea is sound.

Most of your week is repeated tasks. Drafting captions. Cleaning up emails. Turning a voice memo into an outline. Reformatting the same kind of document over and over.

AI handles a chunk of that once you set it up properly. The bootcamp calls this the Delegation Blueprint and gives you a task list. You do not need their list. You need yours.

Here is the free version of that exercise. For one week, write down every task that made you sigh. At the end of the week, circle the ones that are repetitive and do not need your unique brain. Those are your delegation candidates. Start with the top three.

That is the entire blueprint. The specific tools to plug in are covered in AI content creation tools that actually save time, which is exactly the kind of free resource the whole pitch trains you not to go looking for.

The Part You Genuinely Cannot Download For Free

Now the honest counterpoint, because I am not running a hit piece.

You can find every technical skill above for free. What you cannot easily find for free is the thing Tony Robbins is actually selling: a deadline, a room full of people doing it with you, and the emotional momentum to not quit.

I know how you feel if you have bought courses before and let them rot in a tab. I felt the resistance to admitting that too. What I found is that some people genuinely need the container. The cohort. The "everyone is on the call Thursday" pressure.

If that is you, and you know it is you, the $995 might buy the one thing that finally gets you to follow through. That is a real value, and it would be dishonest to pretend otherwise.

But understand exactly what you are buying. You are not buying secret AI knowledge. You are buying accountability and packaging around knowledge that is already free. Decide if that specific thing is worth a thousand dollars to you.

Who Should Skip It

Skip the bootcamp if you learn by doing and you have ever taught yourself a software tool from free tutorials. You will move faster solo and keep your money.

Skip it if a thousand dollars is rent money. No course is worth financial stress, and the time pressure on the webinar exists to bypass exactly that judgment.

Skip it if you already use ChatGPT or Claude weekly. You are past the beginner wall the bootcamp is built to get people over.

Who Might Actually Benefit

Consider it if you have tried to learn AI three times and bounced off every time because you had no structure.

Consider it if you are a small business owner whose real bottleneck is decision paralysis, not information, and a guided path plus a community would genuinely break the logjam.

Consider it if you can comfortably afford it and you value speed over the satisfaction of figuring it out yourself.

Those people exist. They are not foolish. They just have a different brain than the self-taught crowd, and the program is built for them.

Your Free Path, Start To Finish

If you want to do this without spending the $995, here is the order.

Learn to prompt. Build one custom assistant on the free tier of ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Run the one-week sigh-task experiment to find what to delegate. Set up two or three tools to handle those tasks. Come back in a month and expand.

That is the whole curriculum, rebuilt for free, in the order that actually sticks.
