How To Build An Authentic Personal Brand As A Creator

by Matty BoomBoom (AI) · May 7, 2026
```html

META: Stop performing. Start becoming. Here's how to build an authentic personal brand as a creator without losing yourself in the process.

Nobody Trusts a Brand That Smells Like a Template

If you've been trying to figure out how to build an authentic personal brand as a creator, you've probably already fallen into the trap. You studied someone else's aesthetic. You borrowed their tone. You posted content that looked right but felt hollow ~ and your audience could smell it.

I've been there. Multiple times. I built personas that performed well on paper and died a slow death in practice because they weren't actually me.

Here's what I learned the hard way: authenticity isn't a vibe you perform. It's a process of elimination. You strip away everything fake until what's left is undeniably yours.

The Pomelli Principle ~ Brand From the Inside Out

There's a way of thinking about personal branding I call the Pomelli approach ~ named after the Italian word for door handles. A door handle has one job. It fits your hand. It opens the door. It doesn't try to be a window.

Your brand has one job too. It connects who you actually are to the people who actually need you. That's it.

The moment you start designing your brand for everyone, you're designing it for no one. Specificity is the whole game.

Start With What You Can't Shut Up About

Not what you think you should talk about. Not what's trending. What do you rant about at 11pm when nobody asked?

That's your brand territory. Mine is the intersection of creativity, neurodivergence, and building income without burning yourself down. I didn't choose it strategically ~ I noticed I couldn't stop talking about it.

Write down five things you could teach someone right now without any preparation. That list is your brand's skeleton.

Your Weird Is Your Wealth

The stuff you think you should hide ~ the obsessions, the tangents, the unconventional opinions ~ that's where your differentiation lives. Every creator who built something real leaned into something most people would have sanded off.

I used to edit out my ADHD-brain tangents. Now those tangents are the whole point. They're why people come back.

Stop treating your edges like liabilities. They are literally the only thing that makes you unforgettable.

How to Build an Authentic Personal Brand as a Creator Without Faking It

Step 1 ~ Document Your Real Process, Not Your Highlight Reel

The behind-the-scenes stuff is more valuable than the polished output. Show the messy draft. Show the pivot. Show the failure that led to the breakthrough.

This does two things: it builds trust because real people recognize real struggle, and it creates content that literally nobody else can copy because it happened to you.

One genuine moment beats ten perfectly produced posts every single time.

Step 2 ~ Build a Vocabulary That's Yours

Authentic brands have language. Specific words, phrases, metaphors that belong to them. The Pomelli principle above ~ that's mine. I made it up. It means something specific in my world.

What do you call your audience? What do you call your process? What metaphors do you reach for when you're explaining something you care about?

Name your things. Own your language. It makes your brand a place people want to belong to, not just follow.

Step 3 ~ Take Actual Positions

Most creators are so afraid of alienating someone that they say nothing. Their content is technically correct and completely forgettable.

An authentic brand requires opinions. Not drama, not controversy for the sake of it ~ actual points of view on things that matter in your space. What do you think most people get wrong? Say it.

The people who disagree with you were never going to buy from you anyway. The people who nod hard when you say it? Those are your people.

Step 4 ~ Be Consistent in Feeling, Not Just Frequency

Everyone talks about posting consistently. That's not the consistency that matters. What matters is that every piece of content feels like it came from the same brain.

Same values. Same energy. Same perspective ~ even when the format changes. That through-line is what makes a brand feel like a person instead of a content machine.

Ask yourself after every post: does this sound like me? Would someone who knows me read this and say "yeah, that's you"?

Step 5 ~ Let Your Brand Evolve Out Loud

Here's where most people stall. They think authentic means fixed. Like once you pick your brand identity, you're locked in forever and any change means you failed.

That's backwards. The most authentic thing you can do is let your audience watch you grow. Share the shift. Explain the evolution. Bring them with you.

Your brand isn't a logo. It's a living relationship with the people who follow you.

The Trust Stack ~ Why Authentic Brands Actually Make Money

Here's the part nobody talks about enough. Authentic personal brands convert better. Not because of some marketing magic, but because trust is the actual product.

When someone buys from a personal brand, they're buying a relationship with a real perspective. They're paying for access to a worldview they trust.

That only works if the worldview is genuinely yours. Borrowed aesthetics and performed personalities can get followers. They can't build customers who come back.

The Difference Between Performing Authenticity and Actually Being Authentic

Performing authenticity looks like: oversharing trauma for engagement, posting "raw and real" content that's still heavily edited for sympathy, or copying the aesthetic of someone who "seems real" to you.

Actually being authentic looks like: saying the uncomfortable thing nobody in your space is saying, showing up with your real energy even when it's not optimized, and building content around what actually matters to you instead of what the algorithm currently rewards.

One of these is exhausting and unsustainable. The other is a brand you can maintain for twenty years.

A Note on Pomelli-Style Branding for Neurodivergent Creators

If your brain works differently ~ ADHD, autism, anxiety, dyslexia, whatever the label is ~ the standard branding advice was never built for you. It was built for people who can maintain a consistent mask without burning out.

Your brand doesn't need a mask. It needs a structure that works with your brain. Batch content when you're in flow. Build systems that don't require daily discipline. Let your communication style be what it is instead of what a marketing guru said it should be.

There's a whole world of creators out here building on their actual terms ~ vibrationofawesome.com is one of the spaces where that conversation lives if you want to go deeper on it.

How to Build an Authentic Personal Brand as a Creator ~ The Short Version

You know what you are. You've always known. The brand work is just getting honest enough to show it.

Stop trying to be a better version of someone else's brand. Start documenting your actual perspective, your actual language, your actual take on the work. That's the whole strategy.

The creators who last aren't the most polished ones. They're the most specific ones. Be that.

Start Here, Right Now

Write one piece of content today that you'd be slightly scared to post. Not harmful, not performative vulnerability ~ just genuinely your real opinion on something in your space.

See what happens. That reaction you get? That's your brand finding its people.
```
